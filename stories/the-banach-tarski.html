The Banach-Tarski

Imagine a solid sphere. In the rigid world of geometry, it is an immutable object. But in the world of Set Theory, it is merely a collection of points.

By invoking the Axiom of Choice, we can decompose the sphere into a finite number of non-measurable pieces. Through pure mathematical rotation and translation, these pieces can be reassembled into two identical spheres.

Matter is not created; the volume is redefined. It is not magic—it is the limit of our intuition when dealing with the infinite.
