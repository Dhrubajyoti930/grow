100,000 Holes

It started as a simple wooden board—a grid of nails hammered into pine. Drop a marble, watch it hit a pin, bounce left or right, and repeat. Through one hole, it's chaos. Through one thousand, it's noise.

Total dropped: 0

But at 100,000, the chaos vanishes. The marbles don't settle randomly; they emerge into the shape of the Gaussian curve. The Galton Board reveals the secret of the universe: randomness, when aggregated, is the most predictable force in existence.
