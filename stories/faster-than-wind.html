FASTER THAN WIND

The Blackbird shouldn't work. It defies the intuition of the friction-bound mind. By using a propeller to drive the wheels, which in turn drive the propeller, it creates a feedback loop that exploits the difference in velocity between air and ground.

⛵

When the craft reaches the speed of the tailwind, the air relative to the blades is zero. But the wheels keep turning. Pushing harder. It accelerates beyond the very medium that propels it.
