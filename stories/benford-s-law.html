Benford's Law

The hidden pulse of the universe.

In nature, finance, and the chaotic sprawl of human records, numbers aren't distributed evenly. You might assume the leading digit of a random set of data is equally likely to be 1 through 9. You would be wrong.

The number 1 appears as the leading digit roughly 30% of the time. This is Benford's Law. It is the secret weapon used by forensic accountants to catch tax evaders: if your "random" numbers don't follow this logarithmic decay, you are likely faking them.
