The Eclipse of 1919

In the spring of 1919, the world was weary of war, but Arthur Eddington was looking toward the sky. Albert Einstein had proposed a radical idea: gravity was not a force, but a curvature of space itself.

To prove it, one had to see starlight bend as it passed the sun. But the sun is far too bright—unless, of course, it is eclipsed.

The shadow of the moon is approaching Sobral, Brazil. The expedition is ready. Will you capture the proof?
