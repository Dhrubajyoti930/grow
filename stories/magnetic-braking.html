Magnetic Braking

Tap to drop magnet

When you drop a strong magnet through a copper pipe, it falls startlingly slowly.

It isn't friction or air resistance. It is Lenz's Law in action.

As the magnet falls, its magnetic field changes within the copper walls. This creates eddy currents—tiny loops of electricity circulating in the metal. These currents create their own magnetic field that opposes the magnet's motion, effectively pushing back against gravity.
