1940, Bletchley Park. The Enigma machine is a hydra, its permutations shifting with every keypress. Alan Turing doesn't fight the cipher with pens; he fights it with logic wrought in steel.

The Bombe is an electro-mechanical cathedral. Dozens of rotors spin in a rhythmic, clacking dance, searching for a mechanical consistency in the chaos. When they stop, the code breaks.
