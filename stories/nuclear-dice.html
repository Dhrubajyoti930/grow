Nuclear Dice

Active Nuclei: 1000

You have 1,000 cubes of unstable isotopes. Every second, they vibrate in a subatomic dance. If a die lands on '6', it releases its energy and stabilizes, forever removed from the active pool.

This is the nature of randomness at scale. Even though you cannot predict which single die will decay, the group follows an unwavering, predictable curve. Watch the entropy unfold.
