Atomic Time Dilation

Head Clock: 0.000000000s

Foot Clock: 0.000000000s

According to General Relativity, time is not absolute. It is a local experience dictated by the curvature of spacetime. The closer you are to a massive object—like Earth—the slower time moves for you.

This means your feet, being closer to the planet's center of mass, exist in a deeper gravitational well than your head. Over a lifetime, your head ages a few billionths of a second more than your feet.

We perceive this as negligible, but for atomic clocks, the difference is measurable, proven, and undeniable. Gravity is not just a force; it is a time-tapering distortion.
