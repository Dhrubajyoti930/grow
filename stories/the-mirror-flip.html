The Mirror Flip

The Geometric Truth: Mirrors don't actually flip left-to-right. They flip front-to-back along the axis of depth.

When you look in a mirror, you are seeing a version of yourself that has been rotated 180 degrees around the vertical axis. Because your body has bilateral symmetry, this rotation creates the illusion of a left-to-right inversion.

If you were to rotate yourself 180 degrees around the horizontal axis (standing on your head), the mirror would appear to flip you "upside down" instead.
